Strategic Growth & Optimization Playbook for Sales & Marketing Teams
Direct solutions addressing the 4 core business questions
1. Fix Funnel Drop-off at Stage 2→3 (The 60–180s Hook Failure):
42.8% of connected prospects hang up in minutes 1–3. Reps are delivering monologue pitches. Replace standard compliance scripts with a "Problem-Benefit-Proof" 30-second conversational opening.
2. Eliminate Closing Friction at Stage 4→5 (The Final Mile Drop):
63.6% of leads who deliberate for >5 minutes fail to close. Shift from verbal closing over the phone to instant digital closing (SMS one-click link or mobile banking push authorization).
3. Reallocate 100% of Landline Outbound to Cellular (+1,430 Won Customers):
Landline conversion is an anemic 5.2% vs Mobile 14.7%. Reallocating telephone call volume to mobile channels yields +1,430 incremental customers (+30.8% revenue lift) at zero additional call volume.
4. Implement a Strict 3-Touch Outreach Cap (Reclaim 3,385 Wasted Hours):
Touches 4 through 11+ represent 7,635 repetitive calls yielding only 3.1% to 8.6% conversion. Capping outreach at 3 touches and reallocating capacity to fresh leads recaptures hundreds of SDR hours.
